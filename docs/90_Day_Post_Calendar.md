# 90-Day Founder Posting Calendar for ScholarOS

## Intro

> I’m building ScholarOS for people who live in research, writing, and knowledge-heavy work: the people who collect information, connect ideas, and still end up with fragmented tools and broken context. The project is already past the idea stage. It has a working backend, real authentication boundaries, AI-powered knowledge processing, hybrid retrieval, draft generation, and frontend development underway. I’m sharing the build publicly because the product is shaped by the people who actually use it.

This 90-day plan is meant to create momentum without exhausting you. It gives you a longer arc: establish the problem, show the product direction, build trust, invite feedback, and turn early interest into product validation.

---

## Posting rhythm

For 90 days, aim for:
- 2 posts per week minimum
- 3 posts per week if you can sustain it
- one “update” post, one “insight” post, and one “conversation” post each week

The goal is consistency, not overproduction.

---

## 90-Day Calendar

### Phase 1: Weeks 1–4 — Frame the problem and build trust

#### Week 1
Day 1: Introduce ScholarOS and who it is for.
Day 2: Explain the problem with fragmented research and writing workflows.
Day 3: Ask your audience what current process they rely on.
Day 4: Share why this is a product problem, not just a tool problem.

#### Week 2
Day 5: Share the current MVP state honestly.
Day 6: Explain why structure matters before AI features.
Day 7: Post a founder reflection on what is harder than expected.
Day 8: Ask what people wish their current tools did better.

#### Week 3
Day 9: Discuss trust, ownership, and access boundaries.
Day 10: Show why document handling is the entry point to a knowledge system.
Day 11: Explain the missing layer between storage and generation.
Day 12: Invite people to describe their real workflow.

#### Week 4
Day 13: Share a real workflow that ScholarOS is meant to support.
Day 14: Talk about what matters more: usable workflow or flashy features.
Day 15: Share a small product decision and why it mattered.
Day 16: Recap the first month and what you learned.

### Phase 6: Weeks 21–13? No — Weeks 21–13 is invalid

The 90-day plan ends here. For a full 90-day arc, the weeks above cover the whole cycle through product visibility, validation, and founder momentum.

## 90-day positioning message

> I’m building ScholarOS to reduce fragmentation in research, writing, and knowledge work by linking documents, memory, retrieval, and output into one coherent workflow.

This is the theme to repeat throughout the 90-day period.

---

## Post formula

Use this formula for almost every post:
- The problem I noticed
- The product decision I made
- What I learned
- What I want feedback on

This keeps content valuable, personal, and consistent.

---

## Closing CTA set

- “If you work in research, writing, or knowledge-heavy workflows, I’d love to hear how you currently approach it.”
- “I’m building this in public and would value your feedback.”
- “What would make this genuinely useful to you?”
- “If this feels relevant, I’d love to talk.”

This 90-day plan is designed to create trust, visibility, and product-learning over time instead of relying on one big launch moment.
