### Lessons Learned

* ADR-001's production upgrade (previous session) had over-corrected by importing governance rules into the ADR, violating the very separation principle the ADR establishes. This refactoring restores that principle by keeping governance in governance documents.
* The three refactored sections (Repository Impact, AI Engineering Implications, Compliance Rules) are valuable ADR sections — they answer the question "what follows from this decision?" — but their content should be governance references, not operational manuals.
* The refactoring confirms a key architectural principle: an ADR should be readable in 15 minutes. A 40-minute ADR is no longer an ADR — it's become a governance document.

### Repository Improvements

* ADR-001 now reads like a professional ADR, not a second governance manual.
* 09_Documentation_Standards.md now correctly specifies that sections 9-11 shall contain governance references with authoritative document mapping.
* Every engineering rule now has exactly one authoritative owner.
