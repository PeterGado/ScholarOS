# 30-Day Founder Posting Calendar for ScholarOS

## Intro

This is the first post to make as a founder, and it should frame the story honestly and clearly:

> I’m building ScholarOS for the people who live in research, writing, and knowledge work: the people who collect information, make sense of it, and need a system that turns scattered inputs into usable outputs. The project is already at an MVP stage with a working backend, authentication, AI-powered knowledge processing, hybrid retrieval, draft generation, and a frontend foundation. I’m sharing the build publicly from day one because the real product is shaped by the people who use it.

This intro works because it does three things:
- tells people the problem you are solving
- shows momentum without overclaiming
- invites feedback instead of sounding like a polished launch

ScholarOS is already beyond a raw concept. Based on the current repo state, the product has:
- a working backend foundation
- real authentication and access boundaries
- document and project handling
- AI-backed knowledge processing
- hybrid retrieval and grounded search
- draft generation and writing workflows
- frontend development underway

This is the right moment to begin posting publicly.

---

## Posting rhythm

For the next 30 days, post 3 times per week minimum, or 5 times if you can sustain it. The ideal rhythm is:
- 1 insight/problem post
- 1 build/update post
- 1 feedback or conversation post

Keep each post focused on one idea. Use this simple formula:
- what I noticed
- what I built
- what I learned
- what I need from people

## Weekly themes to keep repeating

### Week 1: identify the pain
Explain the frustrating reality of fragmented work.

### Week 2: show the foundation
Show the product is real and has seriousness behind it.

### Week 3: explain the intelligence layer
Describe how the system makes docs and ideas actually usable.

### Week 4: connect to outcome
Make the value concrete: research, writing, decision support, and knowledge flow.

---

## Best founder voice for this 30-day period

Keep the tone:
- clear
- grounded
- slightly technical when useful
- honest about what is still being built
- curious, not polished

Avoid:
- exaggerated claims
- “we are changing the world” framing too early
- heavy feature dumps that feel like a roadmap document
- AI hype without practical value

---

## Core message to repeat throughout the month

> I’m building ScholarOS to make research, writing, and knowledge work less fragmented and more useful.

That single message should anchor the whole 30-day campaign.

---

## Suggested CTA for the month

End most posts with one of these:
- “If you work in research or writing, I’d love to hear how you currently handle this.”
- “If this sounds familiar, I’d love feedback on what would make this genuinely useful.”
- “I’m building this in public and would value feedback from people doing this work every day.”

This keeps the content founder-led, useful, and relationship-building rather than purely self-promotional.
