# Day 29

Sharing the ScholarOS journey has been a reminder that product ideas get stronger when they stay connected to real work.

The production system now has a few concrete pieces behind that goal: a FastAPI modular monolith, a React/TypeScript frontend, Neon PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security, Gemini behind a provider interface, and hybrid retrieval combining full-text search with cosine-ranked embeddings using RRF.

Those technologies don’t solve the product problem on their own. They give me a foundation to keep improving document processing, source-grounded retrieval, and conversation context—and to learn where the workflow still falls short.

The conversations around research, writing, and fragmented workflows have helped me keep asking the important question: where does context get lost, and what would make it easier to carry forward?

Thanks to everyone who has shared a perspective so far. What should I explore next: document processing, retrieval quality, or carrying context into writing?
