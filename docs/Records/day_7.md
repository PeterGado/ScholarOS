# Day 7

A few posts into sharing the ScholarOS build, I keep coming back to one idea:

The problem is not that people need more information. It’s that their information, thinking, and writing often live in separate places.

That separation makes small tasks surprisingly costly. You have to find the article again, work out which note belongs to it, and remember why you saved it before you can use the idea in a draft.

On the engineering side, that means the document, extracted knowledge, and source evidence need to remain connected in the data model—not just appear together in a demo. On the product side, it means those connections have to make sense to the person doing the work.

I’m building toward a workflow that keeps those pieces closer together, but describing the idea is the easy part. The challenge is understanding the details of real work well enough to build something people will actually return to.

What part of your research or writing process feels most disconnected today—and what do you currently do to bridge that gap?
