# Day 27

I’m looking for a small group of people to talk through real research and writing workflows.

I’m especially interested in students, researchers, writers, and other people who work with lots of sources, notes, or drafts—and have had to invent their own way to keep everything connected.

I’d like to understand what already works in your process, where context gets lost, and what takes more effort than it should. For example, how do you go from a source you’ve saved to a point you want to make in a draft? What do you do when you need to pick a project back up after a break?

I’m also interested in how you search: do you remember the exact phrase you need, or only the idea? That distinction is part of why ScholarOS combines PostgreSQL full-text search with semantic retrieval—but I want to learn whether that reflects how people actually look for material.

This is about learning from real work—not asking anyone to endorse a product or sit through a pitch. A short conversation about your workflow would help me test my assumptions and make better product decisions.

If that sounds like you, send me a message. I’d be glad to hear about what works, what doesn’t, and the workarounds you’ve built.
