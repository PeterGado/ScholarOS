# Day 4

You can save every useful paper, note, and idea—and still lose the thread when it’s time to write.

The source is in one place. Your notes are somewhere else. The draft is in progress, but the reason an idea mattered may already be buried in a highlight, a comment, or a note you can’t quite find.

So you spend time retracing your steps: finding the source, remembering what you meant, and rebuilding the connection before you can move forward.

This isn’t just an inconvenience. When the connections between reading, thinking, and writing are hard to recover, useful ideas can get lost—and familiar work has to be done again.

That gap is the problem I’m exploring with ScholarOS. I want to understand how a workspace could help people carry context with them from the material they read to the work they create.

How do you keep your sources and ideas connected when you move from research to writing? What do you wish your current process made easier?
