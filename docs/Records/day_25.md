# Day 25

One of the most useful parts of sharing a product build is hearing how other people describe the problem.

The words people use—and the workarounds they’ve built—often reveal more than a feature request ever could. Someone might ask for better search, but the real frustration could be that they don’t remember where they saved a source or why it mattered.

That feedback also helps me decide which technical investments are worth making. Do people need better exact-term matching, semantic search, or a clearer way to see source evidence? Those are different problems, even if they all sound like “search.”

ScholarOS currently combines PostgreSQL full-text search with semantic retrieval and rank fusion. But implementing hybrid search doesn’t prove it’s better for every workflow; real queries and user feedback are what should guide the next iteration.

If research or writing is part of your work, what’s one search problem you regularly run into—and what do you do when the first query doesn’t find what you need?
