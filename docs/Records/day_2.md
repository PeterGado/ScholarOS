# Day 2

After I got started on my project, a new problem showed up.

My notes lived in one app. Drafts lived in another. Source PDFs were spread across folders I kept meaning to organize. Some of my best thinking was sitting in margins and voice memos I never got around to transcribing. I had the information.

I just didn't have anywhere for it to live, so I kept re-finding things I already knew instead of actually using them. People think the hard part of research is searching for information. 

For a lot of us, its remembering what we have already found and how it connects.

This is what ScholarOS is trying to solve: a place where your sources, notes, and drafts stay together instead of living in 5 different tools.

How do you currently work on your research? Where does your work live today?
