# Day 2

After I got started on my project, a new problem showed up.

My notes lived in one app. Drafts lived in another. Source PDFs were spread across folders I kept meaning to organize. Some of my best thinking was in the margins of a paper or a voice memo I never got around to transcribing.

I had the information, but no reliable way to keep it connected. I kept searching for things I had already found instead of using them to move my work forward.

People often think the hard part of research is finding information. For me, another difficult part was remembering what I had already found—and why it mattered.

That experience is part of what I’m trying to address with ScholarOS: a workspace where sources, notes, and drafts can stay connected rather than scattered across five different tools. The goal isn’t to collect everything for its own sake. It’s to make useful material easier to return to when you need it.

How do you currently manage your research? Where do your sources, notes, and drafts live—and what do you find yourself having to search for more than once?
