# Day 1

My final-year project taught me something I'll never forget: sometimes, the hardest part of research is knowing where to start.

I had the topic. I had the motivation. I just didn't know how to begin.

The guidance I needed wasn't always available, and there were moments I felt completely stuck. Some students hired professionals to help. Some got help from parents and siblings I spent nights researching, reading, making mistakes, and learning on my own.

Eventually, I made a plan, broke the work into small steps, and figured out the process.

But one question stayed with me:
What if academic research didn't have to be this hard to navigate?

That question led me to ScholarOS 🚀, a research workspace I'm building to help students organize their research, understand their materials, and approach academic writing with more structure and confidence.

Over the next few weeks, I'll share the journey: the decisions, the challenges, and the lessons.

But first, I want to hear from you.
If you've written a final-year project, thesis, or dissertation, which part was hardest?

1️⃣ Getting started
2️⃣ Finding sources
3️⃣ Understanding the material
4️⃣ Writing it all up

Drop a number in the comments. Your answer could help shape ScholarOS.
