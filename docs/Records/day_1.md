# Day 1

My final-year project taught me something I'll never forget: sometimes, the hardest part of research is knowing where to start.

I had a topic and the motivation to do well. What I didn’t have was a clear picture of the steps between choosing a topic and producing a finished piece of work.

The guidance I needed wasn’t always available, and there were moments when I felt completely stuck. I saw some students get help from professionals, while others could lean on parents or siblings. I didn’t always have that support, so I spent nights researching, reading, making mistakes, and trying to work out the process for myself.

Eventually, I made a plan. I broke a project that had felt overwhelming into smaller steps and learned by working through them one at a time.

That experience left me with a question: what if academic research didn’t have to be so hard to navigate?

That question led me to ScholarOS, a research workspace I’m building to help students organize their sources, understand their material, and approach academic writing with more structure and confidence.

I’m sharing the journey here—the decisions, the challenges, and what I learn along the way. I want to build something shaped by the people who know this experience firsthand.

If you’ve written a final-year project, thesis, or dissertation, which part was hardest for you?

1️⃣ Getting started
2️⃣ Finding sources
3️⃣ Understanding the material
4️⃣ Writing it all up

Drop a number in the comments, or share a little about why. Your experience could help shape what I build.
