# 30-Day Academic User Posting Calendar for ScholarOS

## Intro

> I’m building ScholarOS for students, researchers, and academic writers who spend too much time managing sources, notes, drafts, and context across disconnected tools. The current workflow is often fragmented: research material is stored in one place, notes in another, writing in yet another, and the real thinking work gets lost in the gaps. ScholarOS is meant to make that process more continuous, more grounded, and easier to trust.

This version speaks directly to academic audiences: students, researchers, graduate students, faculty, and people whose work depends on reading, synthesizing, and writing from evidence.

## 30-Day Calendar
