# 30-Day LinkedIn Posting Calendar for ScholarOS

## Founder positioning

> I’m building ScholarOS for people who work in research, writing, and knowledge-heavy workflows. The problem is simple: information is scattered, work is fragmented, and tools do not help people turn what they gather into something useful. I’m sharing the build publicly from day one because the product should be shaped by the people who actually use it.

This is the intro post for LinkedIn. It is founder-led, credible, and open to conversation.

## 30-day calendar
